## Demo-data boundary

The expanded catalogue's non-sticker unit prices, material costs, suppliers, inventory, contacts, orders and analytics were inserted as explicitly labeled fictional demo/seed data in the catalog seed migrations. They are not approved customer prices or the shop's recorded performance. The approved EGP 2,100 total for 1,000 waterproof vinyl stickers at 10 × 8 cm and the configured EGP 50 design fee are separate shop-approved pricing rules. Do not treat demo values as operational truth.
